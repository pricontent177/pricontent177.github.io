Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. This policy covers Autopilot, private software that Priyanshu Nikam ("I") uses to publish videos to my own YouTube channels (including Founded East), deliver video clips to my clients, and email potential clients. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What the tool accesses

With my permission through Google sign-in:

  • YouTube API Services: upload videos and thumbnails to my own channels, read and update the status of my own videos, and read my own channels' analytics.
  • Google Drive: only files and folders the tool itself created, to deliver my videos to me and clips to my clients (shared with the email addresses my clients give me).
  • Gmail: send emails from my own account and read the threads of those emails, only to see whether the recipient replied, asked not to be contacted, or the email bounced.

Information about other people

When I look for potential clients, the tool reads public YouTube channel information: the channel name, a business email the channel publishes in its own description, and its latest public video title. It keeps the name, email and conversation status to manage the conversation and honour opt-outs; other YouTube data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days. Anyone who replies "no thanks" or asks to be removed is added to a do-not-contact list and never emailed again. To have your details deleted, email priyanshu.work171@gmail.com.

Ads, third-party content and cookies

Autopilot and this website show no ads and no third-party content, and use no cookies or other technology that stores or reads information on your device.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in my private cloud account, and records live in my private cloud storage. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Video files go only to YouTube, my own social accounts and, for client work, the client.

Google policies

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Revoking access or deleting the tool's cloud storage removes all stored tokens and records.
