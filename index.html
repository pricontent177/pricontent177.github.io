Founded East

Founded East is a YouTube channel that tells the stories behind Asia's biggest companies: the bets, rivalries, near-collapses and comebacks that built them. Watch it at youtube.com/@FoundedEast.

Autopilot, our publishing tool

Autopilot is a private software tool that Priyanshu uses to run the channel. It is not offered to anyone else. With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it:

  • uploads and schedules videos and thumbnails on the channel's own YouTube account, and reads that channel's own analytics;
  • delivers video files to the owner, and clips to clients, through Google Drive folders it creates;
  • sends emails from the owner's Gmail and checks those threads only for replies, opt-outs and bounces.

How Autopilot handles data is described in the privacy policy; the rules of use are in the terms of service.
